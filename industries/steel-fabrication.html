Steel fabrication

Frames, platforms, brackets and welded assemblies that are designed to be fabricated, not just drawn. We size members, detail the welds and produce drawings your fabricators can build from.

Typical steel fabrication parts

Structural frames and skidsEquipment frames, skids and supports
Platforms, stairs and guardsAccess structures and machine guarding
Brackets and base platesGussets, mounting plates and anchor plates
Welded machine basesStiff bases for motors, pumps and gearboxes
Sheet metal enclosuresCovers, housings and cabinets
Chassis and sub-framesFabricated frames for mobile equipment
How we help

Services for steel fabrication

Mechanical design

Shop drawings with weld symbols, cut lists and a bill of materials.

Engineering analysis

Member stress and deflection checks, and weld sizing for the loads.

Manufacturing

Cutting, bending, welding and finishing, from one-offs to batches.

Inspection and documentation

Dimensional and visual checks against the drawing.

Points we design for

  • Weld access and distortion control
  • Standard section sizes to keep material cost down
  • Bolted joints where parts must come apart for transport or service
  • Coating and drainage, so water does not sit in closed sections
  • Lifting points and safe handling
