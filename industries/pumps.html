How we help

Services for pumps and fluid handling

Reverse engineering

Worn impellers and casings rebuilt from samples when no drawing exists.

Mechanical design

New parts and upgrades, with fits and tolerances on the drawing.

Manufacturing

Casting and machining, from one replacement part to production runs.

Inspection and documentation

Bores, fits and runout checked against the drawing.

Points we design for

  • Bearing seat and seal bore fits, such as H7 housings
  • Impeller balance, commonly to grade G6.3 of ISO 21940-11
  • Running clearances at the wear rings
  • Material chosen for the fluid: corrosion, erosion and cavitation
  • Keyways and set screws sized for the drive torque
