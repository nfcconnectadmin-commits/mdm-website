Hydraulic power = ρ × g × Q × H. Shaft power = hydraulic power ÷ pump efficiency. Affinity laws: flow changes with speed and diameter, head with their square, and power with their cube.

The affinity laws are exact for speed changes and an approximation for small impeller trims, within about 10–20%. Check against the maker's pump curve, NPSH and the motor's full-load rating before you change a duty.
