Bolt Torque Calculator

Find the tightening torque that gives the clamp force you want from a metric coarse bolt. Choose the size, property class and how the threads are lubricated.

Bolt and joint

75% of the proof load is a common target for joints that may be taken apart, and up to 90% for permanent joints. Stainless classes use the 0.2% proof strength.

Proof load = proof strength × stress area. Preload = chosen % of proof load. Torque T = K × preload × nominal diameter.

The nut factor sums up thread and underhead friction, so real torque values can vary by ±25% or more. For critical joints, follow the fastener maker's data or measure the bolt stretch. Torques here are for steel nuts and washers at room temperature.
