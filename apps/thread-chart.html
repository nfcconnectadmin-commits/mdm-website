No sizes match. Try a thread such as M8, or a drill size such as 6.8.

Tap drills give about 75 to 80 percent thread engagement. Clearance holes follow ISO 273, and spanner sizes follow ISO 4032 hex nuts. Check your tool maker's data for hard materials or roll-form taps, which need a different drill.
