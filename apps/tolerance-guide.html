Tolerance Guide

Tighter is not automatically better. Each step down the scale can add an operation, a slower cut or more inspection. Move the slider to see which processes can hold your number, then carry it into a quote request.

±0.05 mm

Bars run from the tightest practical tolerance to normal shop practice, on a logarithmic scale in ± mm. This is a general guide for features up to about 100 mm. Size, material and setup all shift these ranges, so we confirm tolerances for each job.
