Engineering apps

Free tools you can use in your browser, with nothing to install. What you enter stays on your device.

  • Check My Part

    Open your own STL file in 3D. See its size, volume, surface area and mass, check it is a closed solid, get a rough stock and machining guide, and send it for a quote. The file never leaves your device.

    Open app

  • 3D Machining Simulation

    Watch a 3-axis milling program cut a block. Run a sample job or paste your own G-code, then check the cycle time, material removed and rapid collisions.

    Open app

  • 3D Part Viewer

    Rotate any part from the library in 3D. Change its sizes, cut a section, read its mass in nine materials and download an STL.

    Open app

  • 3D Parts Library

    Browse eleven adjustable parts, including a pump impeller, pipe flange, bearing housing, coupling, gear, pulley and pipe elbow.

    Open app

  • Fits Calculator

    Find hole and shaft limits, clearance and interference for ISO 286 fits such as H7/g6, H7/k6 and H7/p6, with a zone diagram.

    Open app

  • Bolt Circle Calculator

    Get the X and Y position of every hole on a bolt circle, with a scale preview and a list you can copy.

    Open app

  • Thread and Drill Chart

    Look up pitch, tap drill, clearance hole and spanner size for metric coarse threads from M3 to M24.

    Open app

  • Cutting Data Calculator

    Work out spindle speed, feed rate, metal removal rate and cutting power for a milling cut, with starting values for common materials.

    Open app

  • Tolerance Guide

    Compare what grinding, machining, casting, 3D printing and other processes can typically hold, then carry your tolerance into a quote request.

    Open app

  • Sheet Metal Bend Calculator

    Work out bend allowance, bend deduction and the flat blank length for a sheet metal bend, with drawings of the bent part and the flat pattern.

    Open app

  • Beam Calculator

    Check bending stress and deflection for beams and cantilevers in hollow sections, I-beams, bars and tubes, in steel, stainless or aluminium.

    Open app

  • Bolt Torque Calculator

    Find the tightening torque and clamp force for metric bolts from M3 to M36, by property class and thread lubrication, with a full torque table.

    Open app

  • Pump Calculator

    Work out pump hydraulic and shaft power and a matching motor size, and see how flow, head and power change with speed or impeller diameter.

    Open app
These apps give general guidance for early engineering decisions. Confirm results before manufacture.
