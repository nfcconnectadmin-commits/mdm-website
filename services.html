Engineering services, joined up

Bring us a sketch, a worn-out part or a released drawing. Each service stands on its own, and they share one set of data when you need the full route from design to inspected part.

Request a quoteMaterials we use

Mechanical design

Parts, assemblies and machines taken from requirements through concept, detailed 3D CAD and release, with manufacture and assembly considered from the start.

You receive: Native CAD and STEP files, 2D drawings with GD&T, and a bill of materials

3D Part Viewer Fits Calculator

Good for

  • New products and machines
  • Redesign for cost, weight or strength
  • Drawings for parts you already make

Engineering analysis

Structural and thermal finite element analysis, fatigue checks and tolerance stack-ups that answer a specific question: will it hold, will it fit, and where can weight or cost come out?

You receive: A calculation report with load cases, assumptions and recommended changes

Tolerance Guide Fits Calculator

Good for

  • Parts that crack, bend or wear early
  • New designs before tooling or production
  • Assemblies that do not fit together

Reverse engineering

Rebuild legacy or undocumented parts from physical samples, using measurement and 3D scanning, then correct the wear out of the model so the replacement fits as designed.

You receive: A clean parametric model and a drawing ready for manufacture

Check My Part 3D Parts Library

Good for

  • Spares for old or imported machines
  • Parts with no drawing or a lost supplier
  • Worn parts that need an improved replacement

Prototyping

Test form, fit and function quickly with 3D printing, machined one-offs or soft tooling, and feed what you learn back into the design before committing to production.

You receive: Physical prototypes and a list of design revisions

Check My Part

Good for

  • Checking fit on the real machine or vehicle
  • Showing a design to customers
  • Testing before ordering tooling

Manufacturing

CNC machining, sheet metal, welded fabrication and casting, from single parts to production batches, with the process chosen to suit quantity, material and tolerance.

You receive: Finished parts or assemblies, with process routing on request

Machining Simulation Cutting Data Tolerance Guide

Good for

  • One-off replacements and repairs
  • Small and medium batches
  • Parts that combine machining and fabrication

Inspection and documentation

Dimensional inspection against the released drawing, first-article reports and material records, so every critical feature has evidence behind it.

You receive: An inspection report, and material certificates where supplied

Fits Calculator Thread and Drill Chart

Good for

  • First articles before production
  • Incoming parts from other suppliers
  • Parts for customers who need records
